Training & capacity

03 / Skills

SBRT trains the next generation: laboratory workshops on phage isolation, propagation, host-range determination and banking; bioinformatics introductions for phage genomics; and clinician primers on compassionate-use phage therapy.

Member labs host interns and project students through the society’s network, and the phage-bank partnership gives trainees access to characterised isolates.

Policy & outreach

04 / Beyond the lab

The society prepares position notes on phage regulation, contributes to AMR consultations under the One-Health framework, and maintains dialogue with universities, veterinary bodies and industry on standards for phage preparations.

Outreach runs from school lectures and science-festival stalls to public articles on phage therapy — always with the same message: viruses can be medicine.

Networks & diary

05 / With others

SBRT collaborates with phage banks, national type-culture collections and international phage societies; member labs share isolates and host-range data through the society’s directory.
