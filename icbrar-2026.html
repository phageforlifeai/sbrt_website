About the conference

01 / ICBRAR

ICBRAR is the society’s flagship biennial meeting. The 7th edition gathers microbiologists, clinicians, veterinarians, industry and students for keynotes, symposia, workshops, poster sessions and the society’s annual business.

The 2026 theme — “Combating AMR under one health paradigm” — frames bacteriophages as a cross-sector answer to antimicrobial resistance: in hospitals, farms, food chains and the environment.
