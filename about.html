Genesis

01 / Origins

India met bacteriophages early: within years of their European discovery, phage preparations were being studied and used on the subcontinent — among the world’s first experiments in viral therapy. Decades of antibiotics pushed that knowledge to the margins, but the organisms never left.

SBRT was founded in Varanasi in 2018 to gather the researchers who kept working on them: to archive Indian phage heritage, rebuild capacity, and give therapy, ecology and industry a single national table.

04 / How we work

The society runs on collaboration: member labs share isolates, host-range data and protocols; students travel between institutes through fellowship programmes; and every ICBRAR edition pairs senior scientists with first-time presenters.

SBRT is a registered society governed by its constitution and an elected Executive Council — the Capsid Council — whose office-bearers serve fixed terms and report to the life members.
