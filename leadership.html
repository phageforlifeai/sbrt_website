Council.

SBRT is governed by an elected Executive Council — office-bearers and members who steer the society between general bodies.

How the society is governed

01 / Constitution

The Executive Council is elected by life members in good standing at the general body, held during each ICBRAR edition. Office-bearers — President, Vice-President, Secretary, Joint Secretary and Treasurer — serve fixed terms under the society’s constitution.

The Council appoints the secretariat, verifies memberships, curates the registry and directory, and convenes the conference committee for each edition.

03 / Elections

Council positions fall vacant at each general body. Life members may nominate themselves or colleagues; nominations open via circular before every ICBRAR edition.

Between elections, members contribute through committees — conference, training, publications and outreach — convened by the Secretary.
